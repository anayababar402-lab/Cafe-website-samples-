Our story

Small batch. Slow roasted. Honestly poured.

Brew & Bloom started as a two-table corner shop with one espresso machine and a stubborn belief that good coffee should feel like a welcome, not a transaction.

Today we roast in small batches every Tuesday, source pastries from a bakery three streets away, and still remember most of our regulars by drink.

— Mira & Jonas, founders
